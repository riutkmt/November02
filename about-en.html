Designs for a Wedding

結婚式を彩る、デザインのかたち

November 2, 2026

A wedding that brought together 85 people.
The texture of paper, layers of color, thoughtful forms, and the flow of moving images. Different forms of expression came together to shape a shared space and celebrate a single moment in time.

This website serves as a record for the couple and those involved to look back on the wedding, while also sharing the finished works, creative processes, and design intentions with a wider audience.



Publication Notes

This website features designs created for the wedding, published with the consent of the bride and groom.
Care has been taken in handling photographs and personal information, including anonymizing personal details and adjusting published content where necessary.
In addition, the necessary copyright permissions for the music used during the wedding screening were obtained, and the required authorization certificates were issued.



About the Creator : Riu

I work across various fields of design, primarily focusing on graphic design, video production, and web design.
For this project, I was responsible for the entire creative process, from planning and producing the wedding designs to developing the website.
